Study Techniques

Effective Study Techniques

Science-backed methods to supercharge your learning.

01

Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes and take a 5-minute break. After four sessions, take a longer break. This method improves focus and prevents burnout.

02

Active Recall

Instead of rereading notes, test yourself by recalling information. This strengthens memory and understanding far more effectively.

03

Spaced Repetition

Review topics at increasing intervals over time. This helps transfer information into long-term memory where it stays.

How to Study Effectively

  1. Set a clear goal
  2. Remove distractions
  3. Study in short sessions
  4. Take breaks
  5. Revise regularly

Quick Study Tips

  • Keep your study area clean
  • Stay hydrated
  • Avoid multitasking
  • Use diagrams and charts

Key Terms

Pomodoro Technique
A time management method using short focused sessions with regular breaks.
Active Recall
Testing yourself to retrieve information from memory, improving retention.
Spaced Repetition
Reviewing material at strategic intervals to move it into long-term memory.
